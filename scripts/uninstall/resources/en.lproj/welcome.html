Uninstall ClaudeQ

This removes ClaudeQ from your Mac: the app, its background service, the permission to wake the Mac for scheduled tasks, and the installer's record of ClaudeQ. A run in progress is stopped.

Your tasks, run history and settings are kept. To delete them as well, click Customize and select Tasks, history and settings.

The Installer's Install button runs the removal. macOS asks for your password.
